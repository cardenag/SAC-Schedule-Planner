Rules, imports, and boundaries

Schedule Planner keeps a separate draft for each department. Use the Weekly board for visual planning and the Batch entry view for detailed edits. Hard conflicts must be resolved before saving; block-schedule alignment is advisory and may be documented as an exception when appropriate. Final schedule entry remains subject to scheduler review.
